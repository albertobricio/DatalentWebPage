Use these definitions verbatim (translated as needed) whenever a deliverable describes what Datalent does. Don't let each generated document redefine the practices slightly differently — consistency across proposals, the website, and the newsletter is itself a credibility signal.

**How to apply:** link a deliverable's claims back to the matching practice below; if a claim doesn't fit one of the six, flag it rather than inventing a seventh on the fly (any real seventh practice should be added here deliberately, not implied ad hoc).

1. **Agentic AI** — design, deployment, and governance of AI agents that execute multi-step HR workflows (screening, diagnostics, reporting, process automation) autonomously within explicit, client-defined limits, with mandatory human review at any point that affects a person's outcome. Distinct from chatbots or RPA scripts, which require a human to trigger and review every step.
2. **People Analytics** — turning workforce data (performance, engagement, attrition, mobility) into decisions: culture and engagement measurement, bias and equity analysis, productivity diagnostics. Every output states its data sources and validation approach.
3. **Compensation & Benefits** — pay structure design, salary benchmarking against defensible market data, and benefits program design. *(Status: identified as a gap in current site content per `docs/business-audit-v2.md` — represent accurately per engagement reality, not aspirationally; see `.claude/memory/non-fabrication-policy.md`.)*
4. **Total Rewards** — the integrated view of pay, benefits, development, and recognition, connecting compensation design back to retention and engagement outcomes rather than treating comp as an isolated line item.
5. **AI Governance** — risk classification (EU AI Act tiers), bias testing methodology, data residency, and human-oversight design for any AI system before it's deployed to a client — the practice that makes the "Agentic AI" claim defensible rather than just marketing language.
6. **Workforce Intelligence** — forward-looking workforce planning and scenario modeling (headcount, skills, labor-market signal) — distinct from People Analytics' current/backward-looking focus.

**Existing service names on the live site** (for reference when reconciling old and new copy): "Recruitment Predictivo," "Diagnóstico y Desarrollo," "Upskilling & Reskilling," "IA y Automatización para PYMES." These map roughly to People Analytics (Diagnóstico), Workforce Intelligence (Upskilling/Reskilling as a forward-looking lever), and Agentic AI (IA y Automatización, once reframed per `.claude/memory/positioning-decisions.md`) — but the live site vocabulary predates this six-practice model and should be migrated deliberately, not assumed equivalent.
