**Voice:** evidence-led, human, direct. Datalent's own positioning ("menos intuición ciega, más claridad, datos y visión humana") is also the standard for how this system should write. Confident without inflating; technical without being cold.

**How to apply:**
- **Default language is Spanish** for anything market- or client-facing (proposals, website copy, newsletter issues, case studies, FAQ entries), matching the live site's `lang="es"` and its Spain/LatAm audience. Switch to English only for an explicitly English-speaking engagement, or for internal strategy documents (audits, governance briefs, battlecards), which default to English.
- **Lead with the human outcome, back it with the mechanism.** Datalent's existing copy pattern ("decisiones más humanas, más inteligentes y más eficientes") pairs an emotional/business outcome with an analytical justification — keep that structure. Don't lead with technology for its own sake.
- **Never use "agentic" loosely.** See `.claude/memory/positioning-decisions.md` — this word is load-bearing for the firm's market position and must only describe systems that actually plan and execute autonomously within governed limits.
- **State methodology alongside claims.** Datalent's own audit found that its one well-sourced stat (an HBR citation in the newsletter) was more credible than four unsourced hero stats combined. Every generated deliverable should default to over-citing rather than under-citing.
- **Avoid generic consultancy filler** ("synergies," "leverage," "best-in-class") — Datalent's differentiator against Mercer/Deloitte-style genericism is specificity: name the mechanism, the limit, the checkpoint, the source.
- **No long paragraphs of self-praise.** Datalent's own "why us" content works best as short, falsifiable claims ("evaluaciones realizadas por expertos en ingeniería y ciencia de datos, no por reclutadores generalistas") rather than adjective stacking ("líder," "innovador," "de clase mundial" — avoid these unless independently earned and sourced).
- **Every AI-related claim gets a governance companion sentence** — what the system does *and* where the human checkpoint is. This is both a compliance requirement and a trust-building habit; see `.claude/memory/compliance-glossary.md`.
