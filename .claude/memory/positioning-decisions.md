Datalent Solutions' chosen market position is: **the boutique firm that designs and governs agentic AI for HR, built and operated under European data-sovereignty and EU AI Act standards, with senior human judgment on every account.**

**Why:** `docs/business-audit-v2.md` ran the site through a six-persona panel (CHRO, Compensation Director, People Analytics Director, HR Technology Analyst, Gartner Analyst, Enterprise Sales Director) and compared it against Mercer, Korn Ferry, Visier, Deloitte, and Workday. The conclusion: none of the five named competitors combine "European data-sovereignty" with "boutique, human-governed agentic AI." Workday is the loudest marketer of agentic AI generally but is a US-headquartered platform selling scale, not judgment. That gap is real, ownable, and not yet claimed by anyone at the time of the audit (2026-07-02).

**How to apply:**
- **Always distinguish "agentic" from "assisted automation."** Agentic = a system that plans and executes a multi-step workflow autonomously within defined limits, escalating exceptions to a human. Assisted automation = a human triggers and reviews each step (a chatbot, a script, an RPA flow). Never use "agentic" for the latter — this precision *is* the differentiator; using the word loosely destroys it.
- **Every agentic AI claim pairs with a governance statement.** Don't describe what an agent does without also stating its autonomy limit and its human-checkpoint. See `.claude/memory/compliance-glossary.md` for EU AI Act risk-tier language to use here.
- **"European" must do positioning work, not just describe an office address.** Pair it with something concrete: data residency, GDPR-native model design, EU AI Act risk classification — not just "based in Spain."
- **Don't claim scale Datalent doesn't have.** The position is *judgment and governance*, not size. Competing on "we're bigger/more established than Workday" is a losing argument; competing on "we govern our agents the way a European regulator will actually require, and we do it with senior people on every account" is winnable.
- **Compensation & Total Rewards is a real gap today**, not yet resolved by a positioning decision — `docs/business-audit-v2.md` flagged that no page addresses pay equity or comp benchmarking, which every Compensation Director persona treated as disqualifying. Content generated by this system should either represent this practice area accurately (if/when it's staffed) or explicitly flag it as roadmap, per the non-fabrication policy — never imply capability that isn't there.
- This is a living decision, not a permanent one. If the competitive landscape changes (a named competitor adopts the same European-sovereignty framing), update this file and `.claude/memory/competitive-landscape.md` together.
