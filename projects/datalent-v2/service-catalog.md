# Service Catalog — Datalent Solutions

**Objective this document serves:** the formal, sellable definition of each of the six practice areas — what a client actually buys, receives, and can expect, in a form a salesperson or a proposal (`.claude/commands/proposal.md`) can lift directly from. This is the concrete resolution of `business-model.md`'s "advisory-led, technology-enabled" decision: every service below pairs a named deliverable with a named accountable human relationship, never a bare software seat.

**Evidence basis:** the six practice areas are fixed by `.claude/CLAUDE.md`; each service's structure (entry point → design/deployment → retained governance) follows `business-model.md`'s revenue-stream model; internal ownership per service maps to `.claude/intelligence/capability-matrix.md`'s existing agent-to-domain assignments, for consistency between what the firm sells and how it's actually organized to deliver it.

## Catalog Structure

Every service below follows the same shape: **Entry Point** (the diagnostic, low-commitment first engagement), **Design & Deployment** (the core project-fee engagement), **Retained** (the ongoing relationship, where applicable), and **Internal Owner** (the practice lead, per `.claude/agents/`, for delivery consistency).

## 1. Agentic AI

- **Entry Point:** Agentic Workflow Feasibility Review — a short, fixed-fee assessment of one candidate HR process (screening, reporting, internal support) against the genuine agentic-vs-automation test in `positioning.md` Pillar 1, producing a go/no-go recommendation before any build work starts.
- **Design & Deployment:** a full agent specification (autonomy boundary, escalation triggers, human checkpoint, governance status) per `.claude/templates/agent-spec-template.md`'s structure, plus deployment support.
- **Retained:** the Governed Agent Retainer — ongoing monitoring of the deployed agent's escalation rate, override rate, and drift from its original autonomy boundary.
- **Internal Owner:** `agentic-ai-architect`, with mandatory `ai-governance-auditor` sign-off on every deployment.

## 2. People Analytics

- **Entry Point:** a scoped diagnostic — culture/engagement measurement, attrition analysis, or a bias/equity review of an existing process — delivered with methodology and data lineage shown, not just an output number.
- **Design & Deployment:** a recurring analytics build (dashboards, a validated predictive model where warranted) with an explicit bias-testing pass before anything influences a real hiring or promotion decision.
- **Retained:** ongoing dashboard/model maintenance and periodic re-validation — analytics that goes stale without maintenance is a known failure mode this retained option exists to prevent.
- **Internal Owner:** `people-analytics-analyst`.

## 3. Compensation & Benefits

- **Entry Point:** a pay equity audit or a market benchmark for a defined role set, following `.claude/templates/pay-equity-audit-report-template.md`'s raw-vs-adjusted-gap methodology.
- **Design & Deployment:** full pay structure / job-architecture design, benefits program design.
- **Retained:** periodic re-benchmarking (market data ages) and EU Pay Transparency Directive compliance monitoring across jurisdictions as regulatory guidance evolves.
- **Internal Owner:** `compensation-benefits-specialist`.

## 4. Total Rewards

- **Entry Point:** a Total Rewards audit — do pay, benefits, development, and recognition currently function as one coherent system or four disconnected line items?
- **Design & Deployment:** an integrated Total Rewards framework and, where relevant, an employee-facing Total Rewards statement.
- **Retained:** none as a standalone stream — this practice's retained relationship typically nests inside the Compensation & Benefits or People Analytics retainer rather than standing alone, since Total Rewards is explicitly a synthesis layer per `.claude/agents/total-rewards-strategist.md`.
- **Internal Owner:** `total-rewards-strategist`, consuming output from `compensation-benefits-specialist` and `people-analytics-analyst` rather than originating primary data itself.

## 5. AI Governance

- **Entry Point:** an EU AI Act risk classification for one specific AI system (whether Datalent-built or an existing vendor tool) — the fastest, most defensible product in the entire catalog, since it's a bounded, well-specified deliverable per `.claude/templates/ai-governance-brief-template.md`.
- **Design & Deployment:** full governance framework design — bias-audit methodology, human-oversight design, data-residency documentation — for one or more AI systems.
- **Retained:** the Governed Agent Retainer's compliance half — ongoing conformity monitoring as regulation, guidance, or the underlying system changes. This is the practice most naturally suited to recurring revenue, and the primary product for Tier 2 and Tier 3 ICPs per `ideal-client-profiles.md`.
- **Internal Owner:** `ai-governance-auditor`, with veto authority over any other practice's AI-related output per `.claude/intelligence/conflict-resolution.md`.

## 6. Workforce Intelligence

- **Entry Point:** a scenario-planning session for one defined decision (a headcount plan, a build-vs-buy-vs-reskill choice), always producing at least two scenarios, never a single forecast presented as certain.
- **Design & Deployment:** a full strategic workforce plan tied to the client's actual budget and org-design decisions.
- **Retained:** periodic scenario refresh as market conditions or internal plans shift.
- **Internal Owner:** `workforce-intelligence-strategist`.

## Cross-Practice Bundles

Because `positioning.md`'s Pillar 4 claims integration as a differentiator, two bundles should exist as named, sellable packages rather than only ever being sold as six isolated line items:

- **"Compliant Launch" bundle** (Agentic AI + AI Governance) — for a client wanting to deploy their first agentic system with governance built in from day one, not bolted on after a Legal objection. Primary fit: Tier 2 and Tier 3.
- **"Evidence-Based Talent" bundle** (People Analytics + Compensation & Benefits + Total Rewards) — for a client wanting a single coherent talent-decision system rather than three separate vendors. Primary fit: Tier 1 and Tier 2.

## What Is Explicitly Not in the Catalog (Yet)

Per `.claude/memory/service-lines.md`'s own honesty standard, carried into this document: generalist recruitment-process-outsourcing/staffing volume work, and pure org-design/change-management work disconnected from evidence or AI — both are ruled out by `positioning.md`'s "What Datalent Is Not" section, and neither should appear in a proposal (`.claude/commands/proposal.md`) even if a prospect asks for it; the correct response is redirection to what the catalog actually offers, or an honest decline.

## Cross-Document Consistency Note

Every service's Entry Point / Design & Deployment / Retained structure must match the revenue streams defined in `business-model.md` exactly, and every Internal Owner assignment must match `.claude/intelligence/capability-matrix.md`'s existing agent scope — this catalog does not introduce any new practice-to-agent mapping, it only makes the existing one sellable.
