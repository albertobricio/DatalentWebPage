# Value Proposition — Datalent Solutions

**Objective this document serves:** state, per ICP tier and per practice area, exactly what pain Datalent relieves and what gain it creates — structured on Strategyzer's Value Proposition Canvas (jobs/pains/gains ↔ products/pain relievers/gain creators), a standard framework consistent with `.claude/knowledge/consulting-methodologies.md`'s and `.claude/knowledge/marketing.md`'s frameworks sections.

**The one rule this document enforces on itself:** every quantified claim below is stated as a **per-engagement hypothesis to validate and cite with real data once measured**, never as a firm-wide historical statistic. This is a direct correction of the exact failure `docs/website-audit.md` found on the live site (unsourced hero stats — "65% reducción en time-to-hire," "92% retención" — with no methodology or date attached). A value proposition document that repeats that pattern internally would just relocate the credibility risk, not fix it.

## Value Proposition Canvas — Per ICP Tier

### Tier 1 — Scaling PYME
**Jobs:** professionalize a hiring/HR function that's outgrown founder-led intuition; avoid costly mis-hires during a critical growth phase.
**Pains:** no internal analytics capacity; hiring decisions made on gut feel under time pressure; can't afford a big-firm engagement.
**Gains sought:** faster, more defensible hiring decisions; a first credible HR data foundation.
**Pain relievers:** a fixed-fee, fast-turnaround diagnostic (`service-catalog.md`'s Recruitment Predictivo / Diagnóstico entry points) sized to this segment's budget and urgency.
**Gain creators:** a documented, evidence-based hiring methodology the client can point to internally — the first step toward the analytical maturity Tier 2 assumes already exists.

### Tier 2 — Multi-Country Mid-Market Employer
**Jobs:** deploy AI in HR without creating unmanaged regulatory exposure; satisfy an internal Legal/Compliance or works-council objection to an AI tool already in use; comply with the EU Pay Transparency Directive across multiple jurisdictions.
**Pains:** existing HR-tech vendor can't or won't explain its own AI governance posture in terms Legal will accept; pay equity data exists but has never been rigorously audited; no internal capacity to build a governance function from scratch.
**Gains sought:** a defensible answer to "is our AI compliant" that survives a real regulator or works-council challenge; multi-country pay transparency compliance without a Big-4-scale engagement.
**Pain relievers:** the AI Governance risk-classification and bias-audit engagement; the pay equity audit methodology (`.claude/agents/compensation-benefits-specialist.md`'s standard).
**Gain creators:** the Governed Agent Retainer (`business-model.md`) — ongoing assurance, not a point-in-time report that goes stale the moment regulation or the underlying system changes.

### Tier 3 — Enterprise Innovation / Compliance Pilot
**Jobs:** validate an agentic AI pilot (often vendor-proposed) independently before it scales to production; build an internal case for or against a specific AI-in-HR investment.
**Pains:** the platform vendor proposing the AI feature cannot credibly self-certify compliance to an internal skeptical audience; no independent European reference point exists to benchmark against.
**Gains sought:** an independent, defensible governance sign-off; a partner who understands the platform ecosystem without being commercially tied to it.
**Pain relievers:** a scoped, independent AI Governance audit of the specific pilot or use case.
**Gain creators:** a repeatable governance framework the client's own emerging AI/Innovation function can adopt going forward — the licensing/training revenue stream in `business-model.md`'s later phase.

## Value Proposition Per Practice Area

Each stated as a single sentence pairing the mechanism with the outcome — per `.claude/memory/brand-voice.md`'s discipline against adjective-stacking, and each outcome phrased as a hypothesis unless a specific, dated, sourced result exists to cite instead:

1. **Agentic AI** — "We design AI agents that execute real HR workflows autonomously within limits you define, so your team gets the speed of automation without losing the judgment call on decisions that affect a person." *(Hypothesis to validate per engagement: cycle-time reduction on the automated workflow step, measured pre/post with a stated sample and window — never asserted firm-wide.)*
2. **People Analytics** — "We turn your workforce data into a decision, with the methodology shown, not just the number." *(No universal stat is claimed here by design — this practice's value is the visibility of method, per `.claude/agents/people-analytics-analyst.md`'s own standard.)*
3. **Compensation & Benefits** — "We benchmark and audit your pay structure against real market data and EU Pay Transparency Directive requirements, with every figure sourced." *(Hypothesis to validate: time-to-compliance for multi-country pay transparency reporting, per client.)*
4. **Total Rewards** — "We connect what you pay, offer, and recognize back to the retention outcomes leadership actually cares about — not four disconnected HR line items." *(No retention percentage is claimed without a client-specific measurement, per `.claude/agents/total-rewards-strategist.md`.)*
5. **AI Governance** — "We give you a risk classification, a bias-audit result, and a human-oversight design your Legal team can actually sign off on — before a regulator asks for it, not after." *(This value prop is inherently qualitative — a governance sign-off either exists and is defensible, or it doesn't; no percentage improvement claim applies here.)*
6. **Workforce Intelligence** — "We model at least two real scenarios for your workforce plan, with assumptions stated, so a decision doesn't rest on a single confident-sounding forecast." *(Per `.claude/agents/workforce-intelligence-strategist.md`'s non-negotiable against false precision.)*

## What This Document Deliberately Does Not Contain

No composite firm-wide metric ("we improve retention by X% on average") appears anywhere in this document, on principle — `docs/website-audit.md` and `docs/business-audit-v2.md` both independently identified this exact pattern as the live site's single biggest credibility risk. `roadmap.md`'s case-study milestones are what will eventually earn the right to cite a real, sourced, dated aggregate figure — this document intentionally waits for that evidence rather than assuming it.
