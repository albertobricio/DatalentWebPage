# Executive Photography Guidelines

**Objective this document serves:** the direct fix for `design-review.md`'s highest-priority Executive Perception finding: *"Pillar 3 is literally 'Senior Judgment, Boutique Scale' and no Wave 1 wireframe puts a name, a face, or a credential anywhere on the site."* This document is the brief for the photography that closes that gap — specific enough that a single photoshoot could be commissioned directly from it.

## Why This Is a Priority, Not a Nice-to-Have

`design-review.md`'s panel converged independently on this point: a Fortune 500 CHRO's evaluation staff checks for signals of real scale and legitimacy in their first pass over a vendor site, and `positioning.md`'s "Senior Judgment, Boutique Scale" pillar is a claim about *specific people*, not an abstract capability. A claim about people that shows no people is weaker than the claim itself — this document exists so that gap doesn't persist into Website v2 the way it existed on the original site (whose testimonials, per `docs/website-audit.md`, used first-name-only attribution with no verifiable identity at all).

## Who Gets Photographed, and When

Per `business-model.md`'s open validation on current team capacity: this document specifies the *standard* to shoot to, not a claim that a full team roster is ready today. The minimum viable version — consistent with `design-review.md`'s recommendation to "pull a lightweight credibility device forward into Wave 1, even a single senior-practitioner name and credential line" — is **one senior practitioner, photographed once, to standard**, used on the Home hero and the two highest-stakes service pages (Agentic AI for HR, AI Governance) per `page-specs.md`'s own read of where reputational stakes are highest. Additional practitioners are added to the same standard as the practice grows, each photographed identically so the eventual full team grid (`site-map.md`'s Wave 2 "Why Datalent" page) is visually consistent from the first photo taken, not retrofitted later.

## Composition Rules

- **Consistent crop across every subject:** three-quarter length or a tight head-and-shoulders portrait — pick one and hold it for every practitioner photographed, so a future team grid reads as one system, not a collage of mismatched crops.
- **Consistent background treatment:** a single solid backdrop in `color.paper` or `color.paper-alt`, or a genuinely real (not staged) work environment shot with a shallow depth of field that keeps the background from competing with the subject — never a stock-photo blurred-office-with-bokeh-lights cliché.
- **Direct, calm gaze toward camera** — not a posed "thinking" or "pointing at whiteboard" gesture; the goal is credibility through directness, not performed busyness.
- **Genuine expression, not a forced smile.** Consistent with `design-system.md`'s restraint principle — a composed, confident, approachable expression reads more consistent with "senior judgment" than an exaggerated grin, without swinging to severe or unapproachable.

## Lighting and Color Treatment

- **Natural or soft diffused studio light** — no harsh flash, no dramatic side-lighting (too editorial/dramatic for the register), no warm "golden hour" treatment (too casual for enterprise credibility).
- **Color grade consistent with `photography-style.md`'s general rule** — desaturated toward the site's `color.ink`/`color.paper` palette, or a true, well-exposed black-and-white treatment as an alternative (a strong, credible option for executive portraiture specifically, and one that ages better than a color grade tied to a specific year's trend). Whichever is chosen, it is applied identically to every practitioner photographed — no mixing color and black-and-white across the same team grid.

## Attire and Setting Guidance

- Business-appropriate, consistent with a European advisory/consulting register — not overly formal (no suggestion of a stiff, dated corporate-headshot convention) and not casual-startup either. The goal is to look like a senior advisor a CHRO would take a meeting with, not a founder in a hoodie and not a partner in a boardroom photo from 1998.
- No branded clothing, no logo-bearing items in frame — the identity is carried by `logo-principles.md`'s wordmark/mark elsewhere on the page, not by a shirt logo in the photo.

## What Accompanies Each Photograph

A photograph alone doesn't satisfy `design-review.md`'s finding — it must be paired with real, specific credential information, per `photography-style.md`'s "no decorative-only photography" rule applied here at its strictest:

- Full name (not first-name-only, directly correcting the anonymity pattern `docs/website-audit.md` found in the current testimonials).
- Role and practice area (which of the six practices, per `service-catalog.md`'s Internal Owner assignments, this person leads or contributes to).
- One real, specific, verifiable credential line — not a generic adjective ("experto," "líder") ruled out by `.claude/memory/brand-voice.md`, but a checkable fact (a real qualification, a real number of years in the specific discipline, a real prior institution) — and per `.claude/memory/non-fabrication-policy.md`, any credential not yet confirmed accurate is marked `[VALIDATE]` rather than published provisionally.

## Accessibility Requirements

- **Real, specific alt text per photograph** — the person's name and role, not "person smiling" or "team member" — matching `photography-style.md`'s general alt-text rule, applied here without exception since these are the site's most reputationally important images.
- Photographs are never the sole means of conveying a practitioner's name/role/credential — that information exists as real, selectable text adjacent to the image at all times, satisfying both accessibility requirements and `.claude/memory/non-fabrication-policy.md`'s preference for checkable, text-based claims over information embedded only in an image.

## Cross-Document Consistency Note

This document takes priority over `photography-style.md`'s general "photography is used sparingly, Wave 1 has none" position — executive/practitioner photography is the one photography category `design-review.md` explicitly recommends pulling forward into Wave 1, and this document's brief is written so that a single photoshoot could be commissioned as soon as a real practitioner is available and willing to be photographed, without waiting for the rest of the site's Wave 2 photography needs to mature.
