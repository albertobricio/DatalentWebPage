# Positioning — Datalent Solutions

**Objective this document serves:** the single, load-bearing positioning statement every other document in this set inherits. Built on `.claude/memory/positioning-decisions.md` (already decided in this session) and April Dunford's positioning method (competitive alternatives → unique attributes → value → target market → category) as referenced in `.claude/knowledge/marketing.md`.

## The Positioning Statement

Using Geoffrey Moore's classic template:

> For **mid-market and multi-country European employers** who need to **deploy AI in HR without accepting either regulatory risk or loss of human judgment**, **Datalent Solutions** is the **boutique agentic AI and people-analytics partner** that **designs, deploys, and governs AI agents with explicit autonomy limits, mandatory human checkpoints, and EU AI Act risk classification built in from day one** — unlike **Workday and Visier**, which sell platform scale without boutique governance depth, and unlike **Mercer, Korn Ferry, and Deloitte**, which sell advisory depth without a defined, agentic AI product.

## Category

**Agentic AI for HR, European-governed.** This is a category to lead, not merely enter — as of the competitive review in `.claude/memory/competitive-landscape.md`, no named competitor combines genuine agentic AI (autonomous, multi-step, human-checkpointed workflows — not chatbots or RPA) with European data-sovereignty and EU AI Act-native governance as its primary claim. Workday markets agentic AI loudest but as a US-headquartered platform selling scale. That gap is real and, as of this analysis, unclaimed.

## The Four Positioning Pillars

Every deliverable — proposal, case study, website copy, newsletter issue — should trace back to one or more of these four. If a claim doesn't fit any of them, it doesn't belong in Datalent's positioning without first revising this document.

### 1. Governed Autonomy, Not Automation
Datalent's agents plan and execute multi-step HR workflows within explicit, client-defined autonomy limits, always escalating exceptions to a human — this is the precise agentic/automation distinction defined in `.claude/memory/positioning-decisions.md` and `.claude/knowledge/agentic-ai.md`. The word "agentic" is never used for a system that's really a human-triggered, human-reviewed-every-step tool. This precision is the pillar's entire value — using it loosely destroys it.

### 2. European by Design
Data residency, GDPR-native model design, and EU AI Act risk-tiering are structural commitments, not marketing language layered on afterward. This is the pillar that directly answers the Workday/Visier threat: a European buyer increasingly has a real, non-symbolic reason to prefer a European-governed partner as EU AI Act enforcement matures (`.claude/knowledge/eu-ai-act.md`).

### 3. Senior Judgment, Direct Access
You always work with the person who designs and executes the engagement.

**Corrected 2026-07-28.** This pillar previously read "No junior-analyst pools. Every engagement is led by a practitioner with real domain depth in one of the six practice areas." That wording implied a bench of area specialists. Datalent is currently **one practitioner operating as autónoma, pre-incorporation**, so the site copy was corrected during the Sprint 7 honesty pass and this document was left as the only inaccurate source. It is now aligned.

Two things were also dropped from the live wording and should not be reintroduced here: the reference to junior teams (it points at competitors instead of stating our own commitment) and any implication of multiple senior leads. The live pillar reads: *"Criterio senior, trato directo / Trabajas siempre con quien diseña y ejecuta."*

### 4. The Full Talent Lifecycle, Integrated
Six practices — Agentic AI, People Analytics, Compensation & Benefits, Total Rewards, AI Governance, Workforce Intelligence — function as one integrated capability, not six disconnected point solutions. This is the pillar that answers Mercer/Korn Ferry/Deloitte's breadth advantage: Datalent doesn't out-scale them, it out-integrates them, because the six practices are designed from the outset to reference and reinforce each other (visible in this same session's own `.claude/knowledge-graph.md` relationship map).

**Six in the framework, five on the site.** The website merges Compensation & Benefits and Total Rewards into a single page (`/servicios/compensacion-total-rewards`), so it ships **five** practice pages and five practice links in navigation and footer. Until 2026-07-28 the live copy still said "seis prácticas" in nine user-facing places — including the services-hub `<h1>` and its SEO `<title>` — while listing five, because the count was inherited from this document and never reconciled with what was actually built. The site now says *cinco* everywhere.

This document keeps saying six because the framework's practice taxonomy genuinely has six. Anyone changing the site's count back to six must first split the merged page; anyone splitting the page must update the site's count. They are not independent.

## What Datalent Is Not

Stated explicitly, because a positioning that doesn't rule anything out isn't a positioning:

- **Not a SaaS platform.** Datalent doesn't sell a product a client configures alone — every deployed system carries an accountable human governance relationship. Competing as software would mean competing where platform incumbents are structurally strongest.
- **Not a generalist HR consultancy.** Datalent doesn't do generic org-design or change-management work disconnected from evidence and AI governance — that's Deloitte's and Mercer's terrain, and competing there means competing on scale Datalent doesn't have.
- **Not a staffing or recruitment-process-outsourcing agency.** Predictive recruitment is one service within People Analytics, not the whole firm — this rules out the "generalista de RRHH" framing `docs/business-analysis.md` flagged as a residual risk in the current site's meta-keywords (job-seeker/competitor-brand terms like "eurofirms," "adecco").
- **Not a US-headquartered platform's European reseller.** Datalent's governance layer is designed to be independently credible even when auditing or complementing a client's existing Workday/SAP SuccessFactors deployment — a partner relationship, not a distribution relationship.

## How This Positioning Resolves the Prior Audit's Core Finding

`docs/business-audit-v2.md` concluded the live site did not position Datalent as a European leader in Agentic AI for HR, for three concrete reasons: the word "agentic" never appeared, there was no autonomy/governance proof, and "European" described an office address rather than a product thesis. Every one of the four pillars above is a direct, structural answer to one of those three gaps — this document is the fix, not just a restatement of the ambition.
